Rookie mistakes to skip

Building right at spawn. High traffic means high raid risk — walk a bit further out.

Skipping a code lock. An unlocked door is a free base — lock everything, day one.

Leaving loot in view. Sort boxes out of window sightlines — visible loot gets you scoped and raided.

Going in blind. Read the rules and hop in Discord first — most bans are avoidable.

House rules
Zero tolerance

Cheating & exploits

Aimbots, ESP, macros, duping, or exploiting known bugs result in an immediate permanent ban.

PvP

Raiding is fair game

Raid anyone, anytime. Spawn camping and repeatedly targeting new players after they've lost everything is not.

Building

Base etiquette

No honeycombing over public monuments or roads. Respect claimed land signs.

Keep it welcoming

About this Rust server

Untitled RustX is a vanilla Rust server with a 2x gather rate on wood, stone, and cloth — every other resource, loot table, and crafting cost stays at standard Facepunch rates. If you're searching for a PC Rust server that isn't a bloated 5x or 10x server, this is built for you: fast enough to skip the early grind, slow enough that loot and raiding still feel earned.

The server runs weekly map wipes every Thursday at 1PM CDT, with blueprints resetting biweekly, so every player — new or returning — starts on equal footing. No premade mega-bases, no wipe-day advantage for grinders who logged in early during a previous cycle.

Whether you're searching "2x rust server," "rust server 2x," "vanilla rust server," or just "rust server" and want something small, fair, and actively moderated — Untitled RustX is an alternative to the big-name Rust servers, built and maintained by a small team who actually plays on it.
