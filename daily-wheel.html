What is this?

Every day, every player on Untitled RustX gets one free crack at the drop — no purchase, no catch, no subscription. Sign in with Steam, hit the button, and watch the reel decide what you're taking back to base.

This isn't a slot machine and there's nothing to buy in here. It's a daily thank-you for showing up — the same items sold in the store, just free once a day, with the weapon kits sitting way out at the rare end of the odds so finding one still feels like something.

  • One free drop per Steam account, every 24 hours — tracked server-side, not by your browser.
  • Rewards range from resource kits and medical supplies up through teas, Super Serum, and the Minicopter.
  • Weapon kits — AK, Bolt Action, Tommy Gun, Custom SMG — are the jackpot tier, each around a 0.10% chance.
  • Whatever you win gets delivered straight to you in-game the next time you're on the server.
  • The odds table below is exactly what the server rolls against — nothing hidden, nothing juiced.
