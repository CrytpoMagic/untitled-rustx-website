Rules / Reporting a Violation

Case Intake · Open

Reporting a violation

Saw someone breaking the rules? File it properly and admins will handle it — no public callouts needed.

// Intake process
01

Grab evidence

Clip or screenshot what happened. Names, timestamps, and location help a ton.

02

Open a ticket

Head to #tickets in Discord and give us the short version plus your evidence.

03

We follow up

An admin reviews and replies in the ticket, usually within a day.

Open a ticket in Discord
// What's reportable

Cheating

Aimbots, ESP, macros, duping.

Harassment

Hate speech, slurs, targeted abuse.

Exploits

Known bugs used for unfair advantage.

Griefing

Destroying public builds, blocking spawns.
